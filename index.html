Ford Bronco Roll Cage Accessories — What Works

The 2021–2026 Ford Bronco full-size has a genuine exposed roll cage — A-pillars, B-pillars, overhead bars, rear sport bars. This guide covers the accessories that actually take advantage of it: grab handles from the brands that get it right, plus the rest of the cage accessory category explained straight.

Updated: August 2026  ·  2021–2026 Ford Bronco full-size only  ·  Not Bronco Sport

The Ford Bronco's roll cage is one of the defining features of the vehicle. It's not a cosmetic bar welded on for looks — it's a structural steel cage that runs A-pillar to rear sport bar and is fully exposed on the interior. Every bar is a potential mounting point, every span between bars is potential storage, and every exposed section at head height is a potential injury hazard if you don't address it.

This guide covers four main categories of roll cage accessories for the full-size Bronco and points you to the two brands doing it best for grab handles — which is where the most money gets spent and where the quality delta is biggest.

Full-size Bronco only. This entire site covers the 2021–2026 Ford Bronco full-size (2-door and 4-door). The Bronco Sport has no exposed roll cage. If you're searching for Bronco Sport interior accessories, you're in the wrong place.

The Bronco's Cage — Four Areas to Work With

A-Pillar Bars

Front grab points. Primary location for aftermarket grab handles. Exposed at shoulder and head height for front passengers and rear occupants. Factory plastic handles here are minimal.

B-Pillar Bars

Mid-cab grab points. Critical on a 4-door for rear seat passengers. Also at head height — foam padding here matters more than most owners realize until they need it.

Overhead Bars

Above the rear seats. Mostly ignored. An overhead MOLLE panel bolted here changes your whole gear organization approach — dead space becomes organized storage.

Rear Sport Bars

Behind the second row. Chase rack attachment point. Carry a Hi-Lift, shovel, spare, or fuel can. Most chase rack options clamp without drilling — verify tube diameter.

Overhead MOLLE Panels

The overhead bars above the rear seats go largely unused by most Bronco owners. An overhead MOLLE panel changes that completely. It bolts to the existing cage mount points — no drilling, no modifications — and gives you a ceiling-mounted MOLLE grid for pouches, bags, and gear that would otherwise take up floor or seat space.

The practical load-out: a first aid kit in a dedicated MOLLE pouch overhead where everyone can reach it, a trail radio mount, and a soft bag for hats, gloves, and sunscreen that always gets stuffed in random places. Once it's up there, you won't understand how you organized the interior before it.

Hooke Road and PTYYDS are the two most commonly stocked options confirmed for the 2021–2026 full-size Bronco. Both mount the same way. The difference is MOLLE grid layout and panel dimensions — worth comparing side by side if you have specific pouches in mind before you buy.

Roll Cage Foam Padding

Nobody thinks about this until they need to. The A-pillar and B-pillar bars are at head height for rear seat passengers — especially on 4-door Broncos where rear passengers are sitting closer to the B-pillar bars. A hard jolt or an obstacle clearance that was closer than expected and a passenger's head meets bare steel.

Pre-slit foam padding in 1.5–1.75-inch diameter fits the Bronco's cage bar sections. Fire-retardant to FMVSS 302 standard is what you want — standard foam is cheap but it doesn't meet a fire spec, which matters in a vehicle with an engine compartment nearby. The padding clamps on without adhesive, takes ten minutes to install, and costs under $25 for several bars of coverage. Do it once, forget it's there.

Sport Bar Chase Racks

The rear sport bars are the Bronco's natural attachment point for a trail cargo rack — a Hi-Lift jack mount, shovel carrier, spare tire solution, or fuel can rack. Several adjustable chase rack options clamp to the rear sport bars without drilling. The universal fitment caveat applies: confirm your rear sport bar tube diameter against the rack's clamp spec before ordering. The Bronco's rear sport bars run approximately 1.75–2 inches in diameter depending on the trim and configuration.

There's no domestic premium brand in this category yet the way Bartact and Bull Strap cover grab handles. The Amazon aftermarket is where this category lives for now — verify fitment carefully and read recent reviews from confirmed Bronco owners before buying.

FAQ

Does the Ford Bronco have a full roll cage?
Yes — a complete structural steel roll cage with A-pillar bars, B-pillar bars, overhead bars, and rear sport bars, all exposed on the interior. It's a genuine safety structure, not a styling element. All full-size Bronco trims have it. The Bronco Sport does not.
What cage grab handles work on the Ford Bronco?
Bartact and Bull Strap both make custom-fit paracord grab handles for the 2021–2026 full-size Bronco cage bars. Both brands engineer specifically for the Bronco's cage dimensions — not universal adapters. Bartact at bartact.com; Bull Strap at bullstrap.com.
What accessories can you add to the Bronco roll cage?
The main categories: paracord grab handles (A-pillar and B-pillar bars), overhead MOLLE storage panels (overhead bars above rear seats), fire-retardant foam padding (exposed bars at head height), and sport bar chase racks (rear sport bars for recovery gear). Each addresses a different function — grab handles for safety, MOLLE panels for storage, foam for impact protection, chase racks for cargo.
Where can I buy Bartact Bronco grab handles?
Direct at bartact.com. Bartact does not sell through Amazon. If you see Bartact-branded grab handles on Amazon, verify the seller carefully — the brand ships direct only.
Does this apply to the 2-door or 4-door Bronco?
Both. The 2021–2026 Ford Bronco full-size in both 2-door and 4-door configurations has the same exposed roll cage structure. Grab handle sets from Bartact and Bull Strap are available in both door configurations — check the product listing for your specific build.
